Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 9, 2026

The short version: Prowl uses your location and photos only to log cat sightings on the community map. We do not sell your data, show you ads, or create accounts that require personal information.

1. Who We Are

Prowl ("we", "us", "our") is a community cat-tracking application developed and operated by Prasun Acharjee (acharjee.prasun@gmail.com). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Data

We request access to your device's location when you use the app. Location is used to:

  • Center the map on your current area
  • Record the geographic position of cat sightings you log
  • Show you cats spotted nearby

Location data is stored as coordinates (latitude and longitude) in our database and is visible to all app users as part of the community map.

2.2 Photos and Camera

When you log a sighting with a photo, we access your camera or photo library. Photos you submit are:

  • Compressed and resized (max 1080 px) before upload
  • Stored in secure cloud storage
  • Displayed publicly in the app to all users
  • Retained until you delete the photo within the app

We do not scan, analyse, or process photos beyond displaying them.

2.3 Content You Provide

Cat names, descriptions, and sighting notes you enter are stored and displayed publicly in the app.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials unless you explicitly provide them. The app does not require account registration.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Map display: Showing cat sightings and their locations on the community map
  • Cat identification: Grouping sightings of the same cat across sessions
  • App functionality: Enabling you to log, view, and manage sightings
  • Service improvement: Understanding general usage patterns (no individual tracking)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core app functionality.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

4.1 Supabase

All data (sighting records, photos, coordinates) is stored and processed by Supabase, Inc., our backend infrastructure provider. Supabase acts as a data processor on our behalf and is contractually bound to handle data securely and only as directed by us.

4.2 Mapping Services

The map display is powered by Google Maps (Android) and Apple Maps (iOS). These providers may collect anonymised usage data per their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

4.3 No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

4.4 Legal Disclosure

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process.

5. Public Nature of Sighting Data

Cat sightings, photos, names, and locations submitted through Prowl are visible to all app users. Do not submit information you wish to keep private. If a sighting contains personally identifiable information about yourself or others, please delete it using the in-app delete function.

When an animal is marked as available for adoption, contact details (an email address or phone number) voluntarily supplied by the animal's caretaker or a partner shelter are also displayed publicly in the App so that interested adopters can get in touch. Contact us to have such details removed.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Sightings and photos: Retained until you delete them in-app, or until you contact us to request deletion of all your data
  • Anonymous session tokens: Expire automatically; cleared when you reinstall the app

To request deletion of all data associated with your session, email acharjee.prasun@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process requests within 30 days.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit
  • Row-level security policies on the database
  • Authenticated access tokens for all write operations

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you not to submit sensitive personal information through the app.

8. Children's Privacy

Prowl is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted information through the app, please contact us at acharjee.prasun@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of data we hold about your session
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (see Section 6)
  • Correction: Correct inaccurate data by editing or deleting it in-app
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Restriction / Objection: Object to certain processing activities

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at acharjee.prasun@gmail.com.

10. International Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in data centres operated by Supabase, which may be located outside your country of residence. By using the app you consent to this transfer. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place through Supabase's data processing agreements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes we will make reasonable efforts to notify users within the app.
